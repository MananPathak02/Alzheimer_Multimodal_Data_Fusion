FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Research Inquiries

Key details regarding imaging requirements, cross-attention mechanics, and clinical scope.

Is AlzFusion+ a standalone clinical diagnostic tool? +

No. AlzFusion+ is strictly an academic research and investigative framework designed to evaluate multimodal fusion architectures. It is not approved by medical regulatory bodies for independent radiological diagnosis.

What imaging modalities and file formats are supported? +

The system ingests paired 3D T1-weighted structural MRI and FDG-PET metabolic volumes. Accepted formats include standard DICOM (.dcm) series as well as NIfTI (.nii, .nii.gz) files.

How does cross-attention improve early MCI marker detection? +

Traditional single-modality pipelines miss subtle early indicators. By attending structural atrophy maps to metabolic deficit regions, the cross-attention core captures co-occurring neurodegenerative markers up to 14 months earlier.

Can research teams export attention weights and staging data? +

Yes. All inference runs produce exportable JSON dossiers and PDF summaries containing normalized attention weight volumes, confidence scores, and registration parameters for reproducible research.
